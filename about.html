What is HAVKAR?

A unified platform for multiple digital services.

HAVKAR is an independent multi-service digital platform being developed in Italy with a broader European and international vision.

The platform is designed to bring services that people often access through separate applications into one connected environment. HAVKAR combines areas such as marketplace listings, employment, travel, delivery, communication and digital account services.

Our goal is to make it easier for users to discover services, communicate, find opportunities and manage everyday activities without moving between many unrelated platforms.

The HAVKAR Platform

Core service areas being developed within HAVKAR.

Buy & Sell

A marketplace where users can publish, discover and save listings for products and other available items.

Jobs

A jobs area designed for publishing and discovering employment opportunities and connecting employers with job seekers.

Travel

Travel tools designed to help users explore transportation options and access travel services from within HAVKAR.

Delivery

Delivery features designed to connect delivery requests, active deliveries and delivery history in one place.

Messaging

Communication tools that help HAVKAR users interact through the platform, including multilingual communication features.

Wallet

Digital wallet functionality being developed to provide users with account, transaction and supported digital-asset tools.

Our Mission

Connecting people and opportunities

HAVKAR aims to reduce fragmentation between everyday digital services by providing a single platform where people can access useful tools, communicate and connect with opportunities across different areas of daily life.
