Privacy Policy — draft

Draft for the HAVKAR owner and legal counsel. This page is not legal advice and it is not a certification of GDPR or any other law. Counsel must review it before it is treated as the final policy.

پیش‌نویس برای مالک HAVKAR و مشاور حقوقی. این صفحه مشاوره حقوقی نیست و گواهی انطباق با GDPR یا قانون دیگری نیست. تا پیش از بازبینی مشاور، سیاست نهایی محسوب نمی‌شود.

Who this draft covers

This draft describes the free public web app at havkar-app.vercel.app. The legal name, address, and registration of the operator are not stated here. The owner will add those details after counsel review. Questions go to support@havkar.online.

این پیش‌نویس وب‌اپ رایگان و عمومی havkar-app.vercel.app را توضیح می‌دهد. نام حقوقی، نشانی و شماره ثبت بهره‌بردار اینجا نوشته نشده است. مالک پس از بازبینی مشاور آن را اضافه می‌کند. تماس: support@havkar.online.

Information the app handles

  • Account details you submit at signup, such as name, email, password, account type, and preferred language.
  • A session kept by the signed-in browser so you can stay logged in. HAVKAR uses that session storage for the account. This draft does not add a marketing or analytics cookie.
  • Content you choose to send, including messages, listings, delivery details, and files.
  • Privacy requests you submit for export or deletion.
  • Technical logs needed to run and protect the service.

Payment card numbers are not collected by this draft while real payments are paused. A paused payment screen must not be read as an active checkout.

Why it is used

The information is used to create and protect your account, show the service in your language, operate the free features you open, answer a support or privacy request, and prevent abuse. It is not sold in this draft.

اطلاعات برای ساخت و حفاظت از حساب، نمایش خدمت به زبان شما، اجرای امکانات رایگانی که باز می‌کنید، پاسخ به پشتیبانی یا درخواست حریم خصوصی، و جلوگیری از سوءاستفاده به کار می‌رود. در این پیش‌نویس فروخته نمی‌شود.

Account deletion and export

You can submit a request from the privacy request page. Sending the form stores a request. It does not delete the account by itself. Wallet, payment, and other records that must be kept for legal or accounting reasons can remain until a separate approved process handles them. This page does not promise an instant or complete erasure.

How long and who helps

Account data stays while the account is active and for the period needed to handle a privacy request or a legal duty. HAVKAR uses its application database and the host that serves this website. The exact subprocessors, retention periods, and international transfers must be confirmed by counsel before this draft is final.

Your requests

You may ask for access, correction, export, or deletion through the privacy request page or support@havkar.online. This draft does not appoint a supervisory authority and does not state a complaint deadline. Counsel will add that.
