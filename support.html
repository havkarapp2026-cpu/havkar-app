Frequently Asked Questions

Open the registration option from the HAVKAR home or login page, enter the required information and complete the verification process.
Use the "Forgot password" option on the login screen and follow the available verification instructions to create a new password.
Open the relevant listing and use the available messaging option to communicate with the seller. Never share passwords or security codes.
Available payment methods depend on the service and the payment configuration of HAVKAR. Always verify the payment information shown inside the official transaction flow.
You can start a support conversation, contact support using an available contact method, or use the report option provided by the relevant HAVKAR service.
Keep your password, verification codes and payment credentials private. HAVKAR should never require you to send these sensitive credentials through ordinary chat messages.
Need urgent help?
If you believe your account or payment is at risk, contact support immediately and do not share your password or verification codes.
