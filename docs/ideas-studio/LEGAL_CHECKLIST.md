# HAVKAR Ideas & Ventures — Legal review checklist (draft)

Status: **DRAFT for lawyer review**. Not an executed agreement. Not a grant approval.

## Scope
- Italian / EU company law
- Consumer protection where end-users pay
- GDPR / data protection
- Intellectual property and licensing
- Proposed revenue-sharing (net profit), not marketplace listing fees

## Must remain OFF until owner + counsel approval
- [ ] Live venture payments (Stripe / XLM)
- [ ] Public publishing of user mini-apps
- [ ] Ownership transfer of ideas or ventures
- [ ] Automatic appropriation of user IP by HAVKAR
- [ ] Partner payouts under any split (including proposed 70/30)

## IP distinctions
1. **HAVKAR platform** — owned/operated by HAVKAR.
2. **Licensed Studio templates/components** — HAVKAR technology licensed for use inside the ecosystem.
3. **User-submitted ideas** — remain confidential; ownership is not automatically transferred.
4. **Jointly developed venture assets** — require a separately signed agreement.

## Proposed 70/30 net-profit model
- Default proposal: HAVKAR 70% / idea-partner 30% of **net profit**.
- Revenue ≠ profit.
- Net profit policy (draft): gross revenue − refunds − taxes − payment processor costs − infrastructure − agreed operating costs − other documented eligible expenses.
- Split cannot activate without informed partner acceptance **and** owner approval.
- Does **not** replace existing marketplace buyer/seller fees.

## Funding claims
- Any proposed funding (including €500,000 for Aldo's project) is an **unverified opportunity**, not an approved grant.

## GDPR / confidentiality
- Ideas private by default.
- Access limited to submitter and authorized reviewers.
- Audit trails for moderation and venture changes.
- Abuse reporting and retention policy TBD with counsel.
