Seller payout setup (Stripe Connect + XLM)

Sign in to see your Stripe Connect and Stellar receive status. No bank details are invented by HAVKAR.

  1. Sign in with your HAVKAR seller account (Owner: havkarapp2026@gmail.com).
  2. Tap Start / continue Stripe Connect — KYC and bank details stay on Stripe-hosted pages.
  3. Return here until status shows charges_enabled=true.
  4. Optional XLM: register a Stellar receive public key in Wallet (never a seed).

Location: Buy & Sell → directly under the green header (above Search). When signed in, header chip Connect (left of the bell) scrolls here. No live charge from this panel.

▸ Fees & marketplace rules

Publishing a listing is free. At checkout the buyer fee is 2% and the seller fee is 3% (server-enforced). Example: list €100 → buyer pays €102, seller receives €97, HAVKAR commission €5. Taxes, shipping and card-processor fees are separate when applicable. Refunds and disputes for card payments are recorded by Stripe webhooks; XLM direct-split refunds require the seller (merchant) to send funds back. See Terms draft — marketplace fees. Buy & Sell stays distinct from paid Ads. Public live charges stay Owner-gated.
