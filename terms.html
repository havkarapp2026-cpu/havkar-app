The free public beta

HAVKAR lets you create an account and use the features that are actually switched on. Real payments stay off until the owner turns them on. That includes card checkout, Pi payments, Stellar mainnet, money transfer, currency trade, paid tickets, paid subscriptions, and paid AI. A button that is paused must not be treated as a way to pay.

HAVKAR امکان ساخت حساب و استفاده از قابلیت‌هایی را می‌دهد که واقعاً روشن هستند. پرداخت واقعی تا وقتی مالک آن را روشن کند بسته می‌ماند. این شامل کارت، Pi، شبکه اصلی Stellar، انتقال پول، معامله ارز، بلیت پولی، اشتراک پولی و هوش مصنوعی پولی است.

Your account

You must give information that is yours and keep the password private. HAVKAR may refuse or end an account that is used for abuse, fraud, or unlawful activity. Closing an account from your side starts with a privacy request. The request does not itself delete the account.

HAVKAR AI

Text conversation can be available when you are signed in. Usable logo and image generation stays off unless a valid payment entitlement exists, and paid AI stays off while real payments are paused. Do not send passwords, private keys, or other secrets to the assistant.

Acceptable use

Do not break the law, probe or disrupt the service, attempt to take another person’s account, or upload content you have no right to share. HAVKAR may limit requests that overload the service.

Availability and liability

The beta can change, pause, or fail. Counsel must write the final limitation of liability and the governing law. Until that review, this draft does not set damages, venue, or a court.
