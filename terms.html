The free public beta

HAVKAR lets you create an account and use the features that are actually switched on. Real payments stay off until the owner turns them on. That includes card checkout, Pi payments, Stellar mainnet, money transfer, currency trade, paid tickets, paid subscriptions, and paid AI. A button that is paused must not be treated as a way to pay.

HAVKAR امکان ساخت حساب و استفاده از قابلیت‌هایی را می‌دهد که واقعاً روشن هستند. پرداخت واقعی تا وقتی مالک آن را روشن کند بسته می‌ماند. این شامل کارت، Pi، شبکه اصلی Stellar، انتقال پول، معامله ارز، بلیت پولی، اشتراک پولی و هوش مصنوعی پولی است.

Your account

You must give information that is yours and keep the password private. HAVKAR may refuse or end an account that is used for abuse, fraud, or unlawful activity. Closing an account from your side starts with a privacy request. The request does not itself delete the account.

HAVKAR AI

Text conversation can be available when you are signed in. Usable logo and image generation stays off unless a valid payment entitlement exists, and paid AI stays off while real payments are paused. Do not send passwords, private keys, or other secrets to the assistant.

Acceptable use

Do not break the law, probe or disrupt the service, attempt to take another person’s account, or upload content you have no right to share. HAVKAR may limit requests that overload the service.

Buy & Sell marketplace — fees (draft)

Publishing a listing is free. When card or XLM checkout is actually enabled by the owner, the platform fee model encoded in HAVKAR software is:

  • Buyer fee: 2% of the listed price (added to what the buyer pays).
  • Seller fee: 3% of the listed price (deducted from what the seller receives).
  • Example: listed price €100 → buyer pays €102; seller receives €97; HAVKAR commission €5.

Taxes, shipping, currency conversion, and payment-processor fees (Stripe / network fees) are separate and are not invented on this page. Exact live fee figures must match the server calculator in lib/havkar-marketplace.js at the time of charge.

انتشار آگهی رایگان است. وقتی مالک پرداخت واقعی را روشن کند، کارمزد نرم‌افزاری خریدار ۲٪ و فروشنده ۳٪ است. مثال: قیمت فهرست ۱۰۰ یورو → خریدار ۱۰۲، فروشنده ۹۷، کمیسیون HAVKAR ۵ یورو. مالیات، ارسال و کارمزد پردازشگر جداست.

Buy & Sell — tax and Italy / EU notes (draft)

Operator identity stated elsewhere in HAVKAR drafts: HAVKAR DI JALILNIA AMIR, Italy (Crotone). This draft does not invent a VAT ruling, OSS election, or invoice template.

  • Marketplace commission (2% buyer + 3% seller) may create taxable supply by the platform — counsel must confirm Italian IVA treatment and invoice obligations.
  • Sellers may be consumers or businesses; B2C vs B2B rules and Partita IVA requirements for sellers remain for counsel.
  • Stripe Tax is not asserted as enabled for marketplace Checkout in current code.
  • Cross-border EU sales of goods may trigger OSS/IOSS or destination VAT rules — not configured here.
  • Stellar XLM fees may raise MiCA / CASP questions (see Direct_split legal package) separate from IVA.

Still blank for counsel: whether HAVKAR is commissionaire or disclosed agent; who issues the commercial invoice to the buyer; VAT on platform fees; e-commerce OSS registration; consumer cooling-off for distance contracts under Italian Consumer Code / Directive 2011/83/EU.

Buy & Sell — refunds and disputes (draft)

Card (Stripe Connect destination charges): Successful payments, refunds, and chargebacks/disputes are recorded through Stripe webhooks into HAVKAR order/audit tables. A refund does not automatically republish a sold listing. Dispute outcomes follow Stripe’s process; HAVKAR may mark the order disputed/lost/won based on webhook events. Seller Connect payouts depend on Stripe account status (charges_enabled / payouts_enabled) and Owner enabling public live marketplace collection.

Stellar XLM (direct_split): The purchase amount is paid to the seller’s registered Stellar receive public key and the platform fee to HAVKAR’s fee destination in one user-signed transaction. HAVKAR does not hold the customer’s private key. Automated dual-leg refund verification for direct_split is not enabled in the current software; a refund requires the merchant (and separately the platform fee recipient, if applicable) to send XLM back. Platform-collect XLM for customer marketplace checkout is disabled.

Still blank for counsel: cooling-off / consumer withdrawal windows; which party is the merchant of record for physical goods; chargeback liability allocation between HAVKAR and sellers; MiCA / CASP questions for receiving XLM platform fees; governing law and venue; VAT/invoicing for marketplace commissions.

بازپرداخت کارت از مسیر وب‌هوک Stripe ثبت می‌شود و آگهی فروخته‌شده خودکار دوباره منتشر نمی‌شود. در XLM با مدل direct_split، بازپرداخت خودکار دوطرفه هنوز در نرم‌افزار فعال نیست و فروشنده باید وجوه را برگرداند. موارد حقوقی باز (دوره انصراف، مسئولیت اختلاف، MiCA، قانون حاکم) عمداً اینجا ساخته نشده‌اند.
